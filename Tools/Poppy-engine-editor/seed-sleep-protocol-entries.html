🏆 SEED SLEEP PROTOCOL ENTRIES

PoppyEngine · one-time setup

Two entries for the Sleep Protocol — the trophies and penalties from your Notion table. One lets Poppy talk about where you stand; the other lets her excuse days you did not log, so the missed-log penalty does not fire for a week you were simply busy.

These sit alongside the seven from seed-sleep-entries.html in the same Sleep & Rest category and do not touch them. Entries are matched by name, so running this twice updates the two rather than creating four.

After it finishes:

  1. Open PoppyEngine-Editor.html and find the two new entries under Sleep & Rest.
  2. Read them and adjust the wording to sound like you.
  3. Press Export PoppyEngine.js and paste the result over JS/poppy/PoppyEngine-core.js.

Poppy cannot excuse a day until that export is pasted in. The actions already exist in PoppyEngine-sleep.js, but an action with no entry is a capability she is never told to reach for.
