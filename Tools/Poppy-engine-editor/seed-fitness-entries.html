PoppyEngine · one-time setup

Twelve entries that teach Poppy the Fitness Centre — what it tracks, how its scoring works, which words mean "tell me" and which mean "do it". Writes into the editor's Firebase, not into PoppyEngine-core.js, which is generated.

These entries only pull and push. The reads come from JS/poppy/LifeHub-poppy-firebase-fetch.js and the three actions from JS/poppy/PoppyEngine-fitness.js — both must be loaded, or the entries fire and nothing answers.

Poppy cannot log or score a workout. That is done by tapping FINISH in the Active Session, because the points, PRs and level-ups are calculated there. The entries below say so.

Matched by name, so a second run updates them rather than creating duplicates. Your other entries are never read or changed.
